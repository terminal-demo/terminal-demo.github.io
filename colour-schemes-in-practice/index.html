Colour schemes in practice

2026.11.12 47 words Demo Author
Compact Full Guide Half List

Pick an accent, then Dark, Light or Custom. The background on Dark is mixed from the accent, the way Hugo Terminal does it, so every accent gets its own slightly tinted night.

Readable small text is one switch away if you prefer every detail at full contrast.
