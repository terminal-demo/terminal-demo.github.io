Typography and rhythm

2026.08.31 Demo Author
Tag 1 Typography

Terminal-Pub keeps the original Terminal type scale and just tightens the vertical spacing. Here is how the common elements look.

A second-level heading

Body text sits on a baseline grid. Links use the accent colour, bold uses the bold weight, and italic uses the italic face when the font ships one.

A third-level heading

A blockquote is bordered top and bottom, with a > marker in the accent colour.

  • Nesting works
