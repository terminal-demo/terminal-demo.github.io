Terminal-Pub

A Terminal fork with a full colour system, ISO date formats and in-page bilingual tabs. Edit this hero, or switch it off, under Theme settings → Layout.

Colours live under Theme settings → Colors: pick an accent, a Dark / Light / Custom scheme, or set your own background and text. The bilingual tab markup is documented in the theme’s README.
